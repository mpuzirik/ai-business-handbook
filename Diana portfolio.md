# Portfolio Entry — Week 1

During Week 1, I was not able to attend the session, so I did not directly participate in the initial team discussions. After the session, I reviewed the work completed by my team and caught up with our project focus, the AIBS research proposal and the AEL product requirements document.

Our team decided to focus on small marketing and advertising companies in Arnhem and Gelderland, with Reklaame as our initial example. The main question is how generative AI could improve productivity while still maintaining creativity, originality and client trust.

As the Tester, I started looking at our project from the perspective of how we can check whether our platform and its outputs work correctly. For example, I think it will be important to check whether AI-generated information can be traced back to its original source and whether important claims are supported by reliable evidence.

One thing I learned from reviewing the project is that we should not assume that we already know what the SME needs. We currently think that AI could help with tasks such as research, brainstorming and content creation, but these are only assumptions. We need to validate them through research and an SME visit.

At the moment, I still do not fully understand how the agentic platform will work in practice and how we will test its outputs. I would like to understand this better in the next weeks and make sure that the final workflow is simple enough for a non-technical team member to use.
