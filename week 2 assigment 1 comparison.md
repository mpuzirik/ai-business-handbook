# Assignment 1 — Comparing the Three AI Articles

After reading the three articles, we noticed that all of them agree that the US, China and Europe are important players in AI, but they have different strengths. The US article focuses on frontier models, investment and technology companies. The China article focuses more on research, patents, manufacturing and AI deployment. The Europe article focuses on regulation, research and its role as a third option between the US and China.

The articles also contradict each other. For example, the China article presents China as very strong in several areas, while the US article presents the US as the clear leader. The Europe article is much more negative about Europe's position. All three articles sound quite confident, even though they use different measures to define AI leadership.

Another thing we noticed is that many specific numbers are not directly supported by a source. The Europe article includes a list of sources at the end, but it does not clearly connect every number to a specific source. This shows that simply asking AI who dominates AI is not a reliable research method. We need to compare different sources and check the evidence ourselves.
