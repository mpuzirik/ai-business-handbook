# AIBS Assignment 1 — AI Article Comparison

All three articles agree that no country or region can claim exclusive superiority in the field of artificial intelligence. Overall, the United States is identified as the leading power in the development of new generation AI technologies, private financing and computing infrastructure, while China is highlighted as particularly successful in terms of research times, patents and scalable technology deployment, and Europe is regarded to be ahead in terms of regulation and responsible AI implementation. 

Nevertheless, the conclusions drawn about AI leadership differ, and hence different countries appear to be considered as leaders. Thus, according to the US article, the US is the leader in AI, while the article on China talks about both the US and China being the leaders in their respective areas only. On the other hand, the article focused on Europe states that Europe lags behind the two countries. 

The evidence provided by the authors also differs. The European article is full of exact figures and data from different sources, while the US and China articles rely on broad claims about investments, research and technological development without providing any evidence for those claims.
