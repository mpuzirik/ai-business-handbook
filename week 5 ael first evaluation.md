# AEL First Evaluation — Week 5

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this evaluation is to assess how well the first working version (V0) of our desk research platform meets the requirements defined in our Product Requirements Document.

The platform is designed to support the production of evidence-based AI handbook pages for small marketing and advertising agencies in the Netherlands.

This evaluation focuses on source reliability, evidence traceability, Dutch-language processing, draft quality, human oversight and the usability of the research workflow.

## 2. Evaluation Method

We will test the platform using a small selection of research sources, including an institutional English-language report, a Dutch-language source and a source that requires careful appraisal.

Each test will compare the actual output with a predefined expected result.

For every test, we will record whether the requirement was met, what problems occurred and what changes are needed.

## 4. Problems and Improvements

This section will record actual failures identified during testing, how they were detected and what changes were made.

## 5. Initial Evaluation

The evaluation will determine which PRD requirements are already supported by V0 and which remain incomplete.

A requirement will only be marked as passed when the relevant test has been performed and the expected result has been observed.

The findings will guide the next iteration of the platform.
