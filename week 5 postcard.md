# Postcard from 2035 — The Future of Marketing Agencies

**Greetings from 2035!**

It is now 2035, and AI has completely changed how small marketing agencies operate in the Netherlands.

At our agency, AI assistants handle the first stages of content creation, analyse customer preferences and generate personalised marketing campaigns within minutes. Tasks that previously took several hours can now be completed much faster.

However, human creativity is still essential. Employees focus on strategy, brand identity, client relationships and reviewing AI-generated work. AI supports creative professionals rather than replacing them entirely.

One of the biggest changes is that even small agencies can now offer highly personalised campaigns without needing large teams or expensive production resources.

The main challenge is ensuring that AI-generated content remains accurate, original and trustworthy, while protecting client information.

**Our message from the future:** The most successful marketing agencies are not those that use AI for everything, but those that understand where AI creates value and where human judgement remains essential.
